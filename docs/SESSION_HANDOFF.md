## Approved identity and governance
- CHIMERA means **Controlled Hierarchical Intelligence, Modularity, Efficiency, Resilience & Adaptation**. This wording is formally approved; do not describe it as proposed or provisional.
- Foundational commitments protect core identity/purpose and require formal constitutional amendment plus explicit Owner approval. Controlled commitments evolve through documented proposal, impact assessment, review and explicit Owner approval.
- Optional external integrations are adapters, never mandatory core dependencies.
- Recruitment evolves with scale: Administrator coordinates initially and may propose a dedicated HR/Recruitment Manager when evidence justifies; Owner approves appointments.
- Four memory levels: Working, Episodic, Semantic, Procedural. The append-only Chronological Integrity history is a separate layer, not a fifth memory level.
- All persistent employee memory must be governed, traceable and subject to Administrator oversight. Memory cannot create authority or permissions; hidden persistent memory cannot bypass statutes or authorization.
- Incident response: detect/flag, contain, preserve evidence, investigate, correct/recover if justified, and learn/prevent recurrence. Rollback must preserve evidence.
- Chronological Integrity Principle: continuous, tamper-evident, append-only history. Corrections are new entries, never silent rewrites.
- Authority hierarchy and amendment process are recorded in [DECISIONS.md](docs/DECISIONS.md).
- Organizational and project chronicles remain separate; verified and appropriately scoped lessons may transfer with provenance, evidence, applicability, verification status, history and usage. Retiring a lesson does not delete historical evidence.
- The Owner has approved proceeding with the definition/documentation of Administrator oversight and intervention authority. **The exact operational powers and limits are not yet approved**; define them as the next CHIMERA governance task after the v5.4.0 release-first pause is lifted. Preserve the already-approved temporary containment authority for serious threats; do not infer broader powers.
